The Xvault 10K Short Story Challenge
Build a world. Create characters worth remembering. Tell a complete story in 5,000 to 10,000 words.
Prompt
A character discovers that someone they trust remembers an event that never happened.
You can interpret the prompt in any genre. Fantasy, romance, mystery, horror, science fiction, literary fiction, and everything between are welcome.
The prize
The winning writer receives a Founder’s Circle lifetime membership to Xvault Studio, including:
- 500 AI credits every month
- Unlimited manuscripts
- Personal onboarding
- Direct founder access
- Lifetime access for as long as Xvault Studio operates
Two finalists will each receive [150/250] bonus AI credits.
How stories will be judged
- Character depth and emotional impact
- Worldbuilding
- Prose and dialogue
- Narrative coherence
- Originality
- Strength of the ending
The winner will not be determined by how extensively they use AI. The final story itself is what will be judged.
