# Xvault daily conversation playbook

The objective is five useful conversations per day, not a large number of promotional comments. A relevant, candid response can create trust. Repeating a product pitch across unrelated discussions creates spam reports and damages the founder's reputation.

## Daily workflow

1. Run `npm run growth:leads`.
2. Open the newest file in `growth/daily-leads/`.
3. Check the current rules of every community before commenting.
4. Select at most five discussions where you can add specific value.
5. Write the useful answer first.
6. Mention Xvault only when the author asks for tools or the product directly addresses the problem.
7. When mentioning it, write: `Disclosure: I built Xvault Studio.`
8. Return later to answer replies. Do not drop a link and leave.
9. Record replies, registrations, activated users, and purchases.

## Comment patterns

These are starting structures. Adapt each one to the actual post. Never paste the same wording into multiple discussions.

### A writer asks how to manage continuity

> The system that worked best for me was separating established facts from interpretations. I kept the chapter where each fact was established, who knew it at that point, and whether it had later changed. That made contradictions much easier to verify than a single growing summary.
>
> For your situation, I would track [specific detail from their post] chapter by chapter rather than keeping only one current character profile. It preserves the reason behind each change.

Stop there unless tools were requested.

### A writer explicitly asks for software

> The main distinction I would look for is whether the tool requires you to maintain its story bible manually or can trace its conclusions back to the manuscript. Automatic extraction is convenient, but evidence matters because every system can misread fiction.
>
> Disclosure: I built Xvault Studio around this problem. Its World Board extracts entities and directed relationships, and Story Pulse follows emotional changes by chapter with manuscript evidence. It has a free tier, so you can test it on real chapters before deciding whether it helps. I am happy to answer questions about it, but I would also compare it with [a relevant alternative].

Include a link only if community rules permit links.

### Someone describes emotional or character drift

> This sounds less like a factual continuity error and more like a missing emotional transition. I would compare what the character wanted and feared before the change, identify the scene that was supposed to alter those things, and check whether that scene actually dramatized the shift. Sometimes the plot explains the change logically while the prose never makes the reader feel it.
>
> That exact gap is why I started building Story Pulse in Xvault Studio. Disclosure: I am the founder. It maps those observations by chapter and shows the supporting passage instead of treating the AI's interpretation as fact. If you want, I can explain the model I use even if you do not use the app.

### Someone is frustrated with another tool

> What failed specifically: manuscript recall, character voice, continuity, or the amount of setup? Those need different solutions. I would test any replacement using the same three chapters and one deliberately subtle contradiction, then compare whether it cites the relevant passage rather than merely sounding confident.
>
> Disclosure: I build Xvault Studio, so I have an obvious bias. I would be glad to let you test that workflow free, but I would first identify which failure is costing you the most time.

Do not attack competitors or repeat claims that have not been independently demonstrated.

> I am looking for novelists with an active multi-chapter manuscript who want to test Xvault Studio with me personally. I will help import the project, explain the World Board and Story Pulse, and investigate anything the analysis gets wrong.
>
> You can use it free first. If it proves useful, the Founder's Circle is $49 once and includes 500 AI credits every month for the lifetime of Xvault Studio. Credits reset monthly and do not roll over. It also includes unlimited manuscripts, one personal onboarding call, and a private support channel where you can reach me directly. There are 30 seats in total.
>
> I built Xvault because I needed an assistant that could follow a growing novel without flattening its voice or quietly losing earlier context. I am looking for honest feedback, not uncritical praise.

## When someone shows interest

Do not immediately push the lifetime purchase. Move them through this sequence:

1. Ask how many chapters they have and what currently breaks.
2. Invite them to test the relevant feature free.
3. Help them reach one successful result.
4. Ask whether the result was useful and what was wrong.
5. Mention the Founder's Circle only after they have experienced the product or explicitly ask about pricing.
